# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.2.7](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.7/Cadence-1.2.7-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.7/Cadence-1.2.7-windows-x64-portable.zip)

**New in 1.2.7:** with Left or Right Alt as the dictation shortcut, every other press no longer stops with “Couldn’t start recording”, and finished dictation no longer waits for Insert here. Pressing Alt on its own opens an app’s menu on Windows, which pulled focus out of your text field; Cadence now keeps that press from reaching the app. Dictation also reaches desktop apps that draw their own editor, as on the Mac. Includes Google Docs dictation in Chrome and Edge from 1.2.6, the weekly dictation allowance on Home from 1.2.5, Cadence Agent's character from 1.2.4, and faster dictation cleanup from 1.2.2.

**Cadence Agent:** say or type what you want done, such as “Open Word and write a 300-word essay about solar energy”, and Cadence opens the app, clicks, types and writes for you, checking each step. Open it with your own shortcut (Settings → Shortcuts), the ✦ floating control, the tray menu or Home. Escape or Stop ends the task at any time.

Sign in from the setup guide, which opens your browser, or from Settings → Account. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Agent, the daily dashboard graph, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. See the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.2.7/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for the exact automated checks and remaining limits.
