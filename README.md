# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.0.5](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.0.5/Cadence-1.0.5-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.0.5/Cadence-1.0.5-windows-x64-portable.zip)

Open Settings → Account → Sign in to Cadence. Your account includes the connection for
the default dictation and writing models, ready to use after sign-in.
Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64.

Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to
review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Choose None, Light or Medium dictation cleanup, view your account allowance and billing,
and check for updates in the app. Setup follows the Mac's five-step account-first guide.

The installer is unsigned. SmartScreen may show an unknown-publisher/reputation warning.
Read the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.0.5/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for exact
runtime, native editor, upgrade and updater checks and remaining acceptance limits.
