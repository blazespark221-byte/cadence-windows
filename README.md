# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.1.1](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.1.1/Cadence-1.1.1-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.1.1/Cadence-1.1.1-windows-x64-portable.zip)

The dashboard now shows a daily line graph with visible points, dates and exact word totals. Select a point with your mouse or keyboard to inspect that day. Days without activity stay at zero.

Open Settings → Account → Sign in to Cadence. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Assistant, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. See the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.1.1/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for the exact automated checks and remaining limits.
