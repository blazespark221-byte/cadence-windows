# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.2.14](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.14/Cadence-1.2.14-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.14/Cadence-1.2.14-windows-x64-portable.zip)

**New in 1.2.14:** dictation goes where your cursor is. If you click into another field, the app redraws its text box, focus takes a moment to come back, or you switch to another app while Cadence is transcribing, your words are typed at the cursor instead of stopping with “Couldn’t insert text”. Cadence never types over a selection you didn’t start with, into a password field or a blocked app. In apps that don’t describe their text fields to Windows, Cadence now leaves your text on the clipboard as a backup. Includes Cadence in the notch by default from 1.2.13, talking with Cadence Agent from 1.2.12, faster dictation from 1.2.11, chat with Cadence from 1.2.10 and mouse side-button shortcuts from 1.2.9.

**Cadence Agent:** say or type what you want done, such as “Open Word and write a 300-word essay about solar energy”, and Cadence opens the app, clicks, types and writes for you, checking each step. Open it with your own shortcut (Settings → Shortcuts), the ✦ floating control, the tray menu or Home. Escape or Stop ends the task at any time.

Sign in from the setup guide, which opens your browser, or from Settings → Account. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Agent, the daily dashboard graph, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. See the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.2.14/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for the exact automated checks and remaining limits.
