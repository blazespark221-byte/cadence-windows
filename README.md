# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.2.10](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.10/Cadence-1.2.10-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.10/Cadence-1.2.10-windows-x64-portable.zip)

**New in 1.2.10:** chat with Cadence. Ask anything, brainstorm, draft or get help with code in a chat that keeps your history, from Chat in the Cadence window, the tray menu, the floating controls or the notch. Drop text, code, PDF, Word, OpenDocument or RTF files onto the chat, the floating controls or the notch to talk about them; they are read on your PC and only their text is sent. Cadence Agent has a new face and the interface sounds are lower and warmer. Includes Cadence in the notch and mouse side-button shortcuts from 1.2.9, files in Refine from 1.2.8, the Alt shortcut focus fix from 1.2.7, Google Docs dictation in Chrome and Edge from 1.2.6 and the weekly dictation allowance on Home from 1.2.5.

**Cadence Agent:** say or type what you want done, such as “Open Word and write a 300-word essay about solar energy”, and Cadence opens the app, clicks, types and writes for you, checking each step. Open it with your own shortcut (Settings → Shortcuts), the ✦ floating control, the tray menu or Home. Escape or Stop ends the task at any time.

Sign in from the setup guide, which opens your browser, or from Settings → Account. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Agent, the daily dashboard graph, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. See the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.2.10/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for the exact automated checks and remaining limits.
