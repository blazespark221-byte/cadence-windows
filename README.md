# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.2.20](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.20/Cadence-1.2.20-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.20/Cadence-1.2.20-windows-x64-portable.zip)

**New in 1.2.20:** the dictation pop-up stays small while you speak. The pill and the notch island no longer grow to show your words as you say them; your text appears in your app when you stop. Cadence Agent now keeps its conversation on the Agent page in the Cadence window, and existing installs move into the notch once. Settings → Account shows which account you’re signed in with, and the setup guide waits until you’ve signed in, because dictation and writing need your Cadence account, including on the free plan. Signing in ends back in Cadence, without the “Cadence for Mac wants to access Cadence” page. Reinstalling keeps your sign-in. Includes the stuck-key shortcut fix from 1.2.16, dictation with a picture on your clipboard from 1.2.15, dictation at the cursor from 1.2.14, Cadence in the notch by default from 1.2.13 and talking with Cadence Agent from 1.2.12.

**Cadence Agent:** say or type what you want done, such as “Open Word and write a 300-word essay about solar energy”, and Cadence opens the app, clicks, types and writes for you, checking each step. Open it with your own shortcut (Settings → Shortcuts), the ✦ floating control, the tray menu or Home. Escape or Stop ends the task at any time.

Sign in from the setup guide, which opens your browser, or from Settings → Account. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Agent, the daily dashboard graph, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. See the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.2.20/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for the exact automated checks and remaining limits.
