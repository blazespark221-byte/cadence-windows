# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.2.13](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.13/Cadence-1.2.13-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.13/Cadence-1.2.13-windows-x64-portable.zip)

**New in 1.2.13:** Cadence starts in the notch. New installs dock Cadence’s controls in a small black notch at the top centre of the screen, like a MacBook’s. Hover over it for Dictate, Rewrite, Chat, Cadence Agent and quick settings, and dictation, Refine and Cadence Agent grow out of it. If you already use Cadence, your controls stay where you put them: choose Notch (top centre) under Settings → General → Floating control position to try it. Includes talking with Cadence Agent from 1.2.12, faster dictation from 1.2.11, chat with Cadence from 1.2.10, mouse side-button shortcuts from 1.2.9, files in Refine from 1.2.8 and the Alt shortcut focus fix from 1.2.7.

**Cadence Agent:** say or type what you want done, such as “Open Word and write a 300-word essay about solar energy”, and Cadence opens the app, clicks, types and writes for you, checking each step. Open it with your own shortcut (Settings → Shortcuts), the ✦ floating control, the tray menu or Home. Escape or Stop ends the task at any time.

Sign in from the setup guide, which opens your browser, or from Settings → Account. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Agent, the daily dashboard graph, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation prompt. See the [verification record](releases/windows-v1.2.13/RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md) for the exact automated checks and remaining limits.
