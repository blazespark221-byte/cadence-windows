# Cadence for Windows

Speak to insert text at your cursor, or select text, ask for a rewrite, review and replace it.

[Download Windows 1.2.43](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.43/Cadence-1.2.43-windows-x64-setup.exe) · [Portable ZIP](https://github.com/blazespark221-byte/cadence-windows/releases/download/windows-v1.2.43/Cadence-1.2.43-windows-x64-portable.zip)

**New in 1.2.43:** Ask Cadence: in any app, hold Ctrl+Alt+K and ask out loud, then let go, and Cadence Chat answers in the notch without taking you out of your app. It knows which app you asked in and any text you had selected there; you can remove that before sending, and nothing is read from password fields or apps you’ve blocked. A reply can include a draft with Paste and Copy, or a task with Run with Cadence Agent, and nothing happens until you click. Cadence Agent now works more like Claude Code, with its own terminal per conversation, background commands you can stop, and the screen as a last resort. The sidebar lists the Claude Code sessions running on this PC, with Claude’s logo. Names from your Dictionary come out right more often.

**In 1.2.42:** Choose Cadence Chat's model from the new menu next to Attach: Swift, the default on every plan, or Advanced on Pro. Cadence Agent's models are called Swift and Advanced too. The Agent request box has a new look, with the model and access menus on the left and Attach beside dictation and Send. Cadence Agent now always works in the background, so the Work in background switch is gone.

**In 1.2.41:** Cadence Agent can run hidden terminal commands and use supported controls in existing windows. Stop cancels owned commands. Command output and selected app text go to your writing provider; unsupported controls do not fall back to foreground input.

**In 1.2.40:** Dictation runs on your PC. Cadence transcribes what you say with NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 on this PC, so your recording is never uploaded and dictation is faster. The first time, Cadence downloads Parakeet (about 670 MB) in the background, and dictation works once it’s ready; Settings → Dictation shows the progress. Parakeet understands English and 24 other European languages. Dictation still counts toward your weekly dictation allowance: Cadence sends only the number of words and seconds, never your words or audio.

**In 1.2.39:** The same controls wherever you put them. With the floating controls at the bottom, left, top or right of the screen, hovering the glass tab now opens the same panel as the notch, with the same controls (Dictate, Rewrite, Chat, Annotate, Translate, Cadence Agent, Cleanup, Style, sounds, Position, Settings and Hide), springing out of the tab. Before, those positions opened an older row of small symbol buttons.

**In 1.2.38:** Choose Cadence Agent’s model, Swift (every plan) or Advanced (Pro), from the new Model button in the Agent composer. When you’re signed in, voice mode is now a live conversation: Cadence listens and answers while you talk, then does what you ask with the same approvals as a typed request (Free 10 minutes a week, Pro 60, up to 10 minutes per conversation; it falls back to the standard voice if it can’t connect). Cadence Agent can write emails by opening a filled-in Gmail or email-app window for you to check and send (it never sends email itself), and you can add your own tools as MCP connectors in Settings → Connectors; Gmail and Outlook sign-in isn’t switched on yet. Home says how many times faster than typing you are, Translate can search 108 languages, the dictation bubbles move on quiet microphones, and from this version on an Update available window at launch updates Cadence in one click.

**In 1.2.35:** Styles. Make a style for each group you write to, such as friends, your partner, family or work, and paste in a few messages you’ve sent them. While a style is in use, what you dictate is rewritten in that voice: the same tone, length, punctuation, emoji and slang, with everything you said kept. Choose Off to go back to your cleanup level. Switch in one click from the Style row in the notch or the tray menu; the main window has a new Styles page to add and edit them, and the dictation popup names the style while you speak. A styled dictation counts as one writing request, like cleanup.

**In 1.2.34:** Annotate and Translate keep your normal mouse pointer instead of turning it into a glowing rainbow ring. Click and drag to circle something, as before; the rainbow line and its sparkles haven’t changed.

**In 1.2.33:** Annotate and Translate work again. In 1.2.29 they stopped with “Cadence couldn’t capture your screen” on every PC. Chat, Cadence Agent (including its spoken replies), Annotate and Translate now share a weekly allowance: 20 requests on Free and 1,000 on Pro (fair use), resetting every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Dictation, dictation cleanup and Refine don’t count toward it. When it runs out, Cadence tells you when it resets instead of “Cadence can’t help with that message”, and Settings → Account shows how many you’ve used this week. 1.2.31 and 1.2.32 weren’t published on their own; both are in 1.2.33.

**In 1.2.29:** circle anything on your screen and ask Cadence about it. Press Ctrl+Alt+C (or choose Annotate in the notch, the floating controls or the tray), draw a loop around a question, paragraph or error message, then type or say your question. The answer comes from Cadence Chat. Windows’ own text recognition reads the circled words on your PC; only those words and your question are sent. Translate works the same way: press Ctrl+Alt+T, circle the text, and the translation appears in a card out of the notch, ready to copy. Chat now opens out of the notch, like Refine and Cadence Agent. A new six-step welcome guide includes a microphone test that tells you whether your microphone works, was too quiet or isn’t available. The app has a quieter graphite look, with Windows’ own icons in the sidebar, and the Release notes button is gone. 1.2.25 to 1.2.28 weren’t published on their own; everything they added is in 1.2.29.

**Since 1.2.20:** dictation waits instead of failing when Cadence is still finishing your previous one, and Try again sends the same words (1.2.24). Home shows the time you’ve saved more simply, with two bars comparing how fast you write with Cadence and how fast you type (1.2.23). When Cadence can’t put your dictation or a Refine result into the app you’re using, it copies the text to your clipboard straight away, so you can press Ctrl+V (1.2.22). Change your dictation shortcut right where you see it: click the shortcut under “Your voice, anywhere” in the Cadence window, then press the new one (1.2.21). The dictation pop-up stays small while you speak. The pill and the notch island no longer grow to show your words as you say them; your text appears in your app when you stop. Cadence Agent now keeps its conversation on the Agent page in the Cadence window, and existing installs move into the notch once. Settings → Account shows which account you’re signed in with, and the setup guide waits until you’ve signed in, because dictation and writing need your Cadence account, including on the free plan. Signing in ends back in Cadence, without the “Cadence for Mac wants to access Cadence” page. Reinstalling keeps your sign-in. Includes the stuck-key shortcut fix from 1.2.16, dictation with a picture on your clipboard from 1.2.15, dictation at the cursor from 1.2.14, Cadence in the notch by default from 1.2.13 and talking with Cadence Agent from 1.2.12.

**Cadence Agent:** say or type what you want done, such as “Open Word and write a 300-word essay about solar energy”, and Cadence opens the app, clicks, types and writes for you, checking each step. Open it with your own shortcut (Settings → Shortcuts), the notch or floating controls, the tray menu or Home. Escape or Stop ends the task at any time.

Sign in from the setup guide, which opens your browser, or from Settings → Account. Required runtimes are included. Windows 10 version 2004+ and Windows 11 x64. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space to dictate; release to insert. Select text and press Ctrl+Alt+R to review a rewrite. Escape cancels. Upgrades preserve your preferences.

Includes live dictation, Cadence Agent, the daily dashboard graph, floating quick controls and in-app updates. Your account provides the managed speech and writing connection.
