# Cadence for Windows 1.2.53: a new look for the main window

New look for the main window: the same features, reorganised and easier to read.

- **Today opens first.** Everything you've dictated, newest first, under one line about today: how many words and dictations, about how long they'd have taken to type, and the apps you used most. Search, the app filter, Copy and Delete work as before.
- **A clearer sidebar.** Pages are grouped into Speak (Today, Styles, Words), Write with AI (Refine, Chat) and You (Your week, Settings). Your dictation and Refine shortcuts sit at the top; click the dictation key to change it, as before. This week's allowance, your plan and any setup still to finish sit at the bottom.
- **Styles** shows each style as a card with one of its example messages. Select one to see its tone notes and examples below, use it, or edit it.
- **Words** (the dictionary) has All, In use and Off filters and a Quick add box beside the list.
- **Refine** has its own page: how the review panel works, with your shortcut, and your presets, each one a click away from editing.
- **Your week** (previously Home) shows words, time saved, dictations and how much faster than typing, a daily chart with today highlighted, this week's allowance, and which apps you dictated into.
- New colours and type in light and dark, also in Settings.
- Your settings, sign-in, History, dictionary, styles, presets and chats are kept. Includes all fixes from 1.2.52.

Includes the required runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for exact checks and remaining limits.
