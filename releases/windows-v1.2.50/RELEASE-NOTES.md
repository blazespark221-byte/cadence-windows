# Cadence for Windows 1.2.50: dictation in more than 90 languages, including Chinese

- **Dictation understands more than 90 languages, including Chinese and English,** and you can mix two in one sentence. Detect automatically works for any of them; Settings → Dictation lists them by name.
- **Your words are ready about half a second after you stop:** Cadence's service transcribes your voice while you speak, with nothing to download first.
- **The old on-device speech model is removed,** freeing about 670 MB. Your recording now goes to Cadence's service for transcription; names in your Dictionary are still spelled your way, and "Clean up on this PC" keeps working.
- Your settings, sign-in, History and chats are kept. Includes all features from 1.2.49.

Includes the required runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for exact checks and remaining limits.
