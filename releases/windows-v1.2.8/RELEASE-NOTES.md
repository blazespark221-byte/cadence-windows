# Cadence for Windows 1.2.8 — add files to Refine

- **Add files to Refine:** drag files onto the Refine window or click **Add file**, then say what you want: summarise a PDF, answer a letter, pull the numbers from a report into your selection, or draft from your notes. Cadence reads text, PDF, Word (.docx), OpenDocument and RTF files on your PC and sends only their text with your instruction. Up to 5 files; very long files are shortened to fit. A result written from files always waits for your review, even with a preset you trust to apply directly.
- Scanned PDFs, images and older Word (.doc) files can’t be read yet; Cadence says so and suggests what to add instead.
- Includes the Alt shortcut focus fix from 1.2.7, Google Docs dictation in Chrome and Edge from 1.2.6 and the weekly dictation allowance on Home from 1.2.5.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
