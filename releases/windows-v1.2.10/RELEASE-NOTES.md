# Cadence for Windows 1.2.10 — chat with Cadence

- **Chat with Cadence:** ask anything, brainstorm, draft, translate or get help with code in a chat that keeps your history. Open **Chat** in the Cadence window, **Chat with Cadence…** in the tray menu, or the Chat button on the floating controls and in the notch. Replies stream in with headings, lists, tables and code you can copy; stop a reply, try again or regenerate it.
- **Talk about your files:** drop text, code, PDF, Word (.docx), OpenDocument or RTF files onto the chat window, the floating controls or the notch, or click Attach. Up to six files per message; they are read on your PC and only their text is sent.
- Chats are saved on your PC (newest 300). Turn this off or delete them all in Settings → Privacy → Chat. Each reply uses your account’s writing allowance.
- Cadence Agent has a new face: bigger eyes and no mouth.
- New lower, warmer interface sounds.
- Includes Cadence in the notch and mouse side-button shortcuts from 1.2.9, and files in Refine from 1.2.8.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
