# Cadence for Windows 1.2.29 — Annotate, Translate, Chat in the notch and a new welcome guide

1.2.25 to 1.2.28 weren't published on their own. Everything they added is in this release.

**Annotate: circle anything on your screen and ask Cadence**
- Press Ctrl+Alt+C, or choose Annotate in the notch, the floating controls or the tray menu. The screen holds still, your pointer becomes a glowing pen, and you draw a loop around a question, a paragraph, an error message or anything else with words in it.
- A question box opens next to what you circled, with quick picks: Answer it, Explain, Summarize and Translate. Type your question, or press the microphone and ask out loud; a pause sends it. Turn on Voice in the top bar to start listening as soon as you finish circling.
- The answer comes from Cadence Chat, in a new chat that opens out of the notch, so you can keep the conversation going.
- Windows' own text recognition reads the circled words on your PC. Only those words and your question are sent; the picture of your screen never leaves your computer. Pictures without words can't be described yet.

**Translate: circle text and read it in your language**
- Press Ctrl+Alt+T (or choose Translate in the notch, the floating controls or the tray), circle the text, and the translation appears in a card out of the notch, ready to Copy.
- Pick another language on the card and it translates again; Cadence remembers your choice. It starts with your Windows language.
- Change the Annotate and Translate shortcuts, Translate's language and voice mode in Settings → Shortcuts. If Ctrl+Alt+C or Ctrl+Alt+T is already one of your shortcuts, Cadence picks the next free key. Escape closes either one at any time.

**Chat opens in the notch**
- With Cadence in the notch, Chat in the notch controls (or files dropped on the notch) opens Chat right there, the way Refine and Cadence Agent do. Your conversation, formatted replies, files, Copy, Regenerate, Stop and Try again are all there.
- Click the chat's name for a new chat or a recent one. Expand continues in the Chat window and takes the message you were writing along.
- Escape stops a reply, then closes the chat; your unsent message is still there next time.

**A new welcome guide and microphone test**
- Six steps, listed on the left so you can go back to any of them: what Cadence does, your account, your microphone, your shortcuts, Cadence Chat and Cadence Agent, then a practice rewrite. Click a shortcut and press the keys you want to change it.
- The microphone test draws what it hears over five seconds, then tells you whether your microphone works, was too quiet or isn't available, with a link to the right Windows setting. Nothing is transcribed, sent or saved. It's the same test in Settings → Dictation and Settings → Permissions.

**A quieter look**
- Home's heading is now Activity, with the dates your figures cover. Pages say what they're for, without taglines.
- Darker graphite surfaces with fine edges, large figures set light, thinner bars and a finer daily chart. The sidebar uses Windows' own icons.
- Check for updates is a quiet button that only stands out when an update is waiting. The Release notes button is gone from the bottom of the sidebar.

Includes everything from 1.2.24: dictation waits instead of failing when Cadence is busy.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
