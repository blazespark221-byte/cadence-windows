# Cadence for Windows 1.2.9 — Cadence in the notch

- **Cadence in the notch:** choose **Notch (top centre)** for the floating controls in General settings, or drag the glass tab's handle to the top centre of your screen. Cadence draws a small black notch there, like a MacBook's. Hover over it (or click it) and the controls spring out: Dictate, Rewrite, Cadence Agent, cleanup, sounds and position. While docked, dictation progress, Refine and Cadence Agent also grow out of the notch, as on the Mac. The notch steps aside while a full-screen app is in front.
- **Mouse side buttons as shortcuts:** choose a mouse side button (Mouse 4 or Mouse 5) as a Cadence shortcut in Settings → Shortcuts. The press starts Cadence without also making the app under the cursor go Back or Forward.
- Includes files in Refine from 1.2.8, the Alt shortcut focus fix from 1.2.7, Google Docs dictation in Chrome and Edge from 1.2.6 and the weekly dictation allowance on Home from 1.2.5.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
