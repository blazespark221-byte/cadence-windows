# Cadence for Windows 1.2.40 — dictation on your PC with Parakeet

- **Dictation now runs on your PC.** Cadence transcribes what you say with NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 on this PC, so your recording is never uploaded and dictation is faster. The first time, Cadence downloads Parakeet (about 670 MB) in the background, and dictation works once it's ready; Settings → Dictation shows the progress, with Download now on a metered connection.
- Parakeet understands English and 24 other European languages, and the spoken-language list now shows those. With Detect automatically, speech in another language isn't inserted.
- Dictation still counts toward your weekly dictation allowance. Cadence sends only the number of words and seconds, never your words or audio.
- Includes everything from 1.2.39.

Includes the required .NET, Swift, ONNX Runtime and Visual C++ runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
