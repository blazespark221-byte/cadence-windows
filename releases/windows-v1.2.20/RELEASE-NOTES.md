# Cadence for Windows 1.2.20 — the dictation pop-up stays small

- **The dictation pop-up stays small while you speak.** The pill at the bottom of the screen and the island in the notch no longer grow to show your words as you say them. While you're recording they show the app you're dictating into and the waveform; your text appears in that app when you stop. Dictation is just as fast as before.
- **Cadence Agent stays in the Cadence window.** A request on the Agent page now keeps the whole conversation on the page (what you asked, each step and the answer) instead of hiding the window and opening a separate pop-up. The app the agent works in comes to the front while it acts. The shortcut, notch, tray and floating controls still open the Agent's own panel.
- **Cadence moves into the notch.** If you already use Cadence, your controls move into the notch at the top centre of the screen once. To put them somewhere else, choose another position under Settings → General → Floating control position.
- **Signing in ends back in Cadence, and an account is required.** When your browser hands the sign-in back, the welcome guide or Settings comes to the front, and the "Cadence for Mac wants to access Cadence" approval page is gone. You need to sign in to use Cadence, including the free plan, and Settings → Account shows which email you're signed in with. Reinstalling keeps your sign-in.
- **Clearer usage messages and rounded Refine buttons.** A busy service now says "Cadence is still finishing another request. Try again in a moment." instead of sending you to your plan. Refine's buttons have rounded corners like on the Mac.
- Includes the stuck-key shortcut fix from 1.2.16, dictation with a picture on your clipboard from 1.2.15 and dictation at the cursor from 1.2.14.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
