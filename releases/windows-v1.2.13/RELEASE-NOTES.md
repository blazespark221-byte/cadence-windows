# Cadence for Windows 1.2.13 — Cadence starts in the notch

- **Cadence in the notch by default:** new installs dock Cadence's controls in a notch at the top centre of the screen, like a MacBook's. Hover over the notch (or click it) for Dictate, Rewrite, Chat, Cadence Agent and quick settings. Dictation, Refine and Cadence Agent also grow out of the notch.
- If you already use Cadence, nothing moves: your controls stay where you put them. To try the notch, choose **Notch (top centre)** under Settings → General → Floating control position, or drag the glass tab's handle to the top centre of the screen. To go back, choose another position there or in the notch panel.
- Includes Cadence Agent voice conversation from 1.2.12, faster dictation from 1.2.11 and Chat with Cadence from 1.2.10.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
