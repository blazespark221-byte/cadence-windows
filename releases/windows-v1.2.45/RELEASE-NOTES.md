# Cadence for Windows 1.2.45: Cleanup & formatting, and cleanup on this PC

- **Cleanup & formatting:** dictate a list and it comes out as a list; dictate an email and it comes out with its greeting, paragraphs and sign-off; long messages are split into paragraphs. Saying “new line” or “new paragraph” works too. The Cleanup setting is now called Cleanup & formatting.
- **Clean up on this PC:** at Light, English dictation is cleaned up and laid out on your PC with S1-mini by Superwhisper, so it’s faster and the text isn’t sent anywhere. It’s on by default in Settings → Dictation. The first time, Cadence downloads S1-mini (about 406 MB) once the speech model is ready, on an unmetered connection. Medium, styles, other languages, dictations with spoken numbers or email addresses, and anything it can’t do well still use Cadence’s writing service, as before. While S1-mini is loaded, Cadence uses about 0.9 GB more memory; turn the setting off to free it.
- **Chat and Cadence Agent open out of the floating controls wherever they are:** with the controls at the bottom, left, top or right, Chat, Cadence Agent’s request panel and its progress card now grow out of the glass tab on the same black panel as the notch, instead of opening separate windows. Ask Cadence answers there too.
- Includes all features from 1.2.44.

Includes the required runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for exact checks and remaining limits.
