# Cadence for Windows 1.2.21 — change your shortcut from the sidebar

- **Change your dictation shortcut right where you see it.** The shortcut under "Your voice, anywhere" in the Cadence window is now a button. Click it, then press the new shortcut: a key combination such as Ctrl + Shift + K, a lone Ctrl, Alt or Shift, or Mouse 4 or Mouse 5. It saves straight away. Press Escape, or click it again, to keep the one you have. If another app or one of your other Cadence shortcuts already uses your choice, Cadence says so and keeps the old shortcut. Settings → Shortcuts works as before.
- Includes everything from 1.2.20: the dictation pop-up stays small, Cadence Agent stays in the Cadence window, and signing in returns to Cadence.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
