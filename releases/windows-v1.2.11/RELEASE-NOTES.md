# Cadence for Windows 1.2.11 — faster dictation

- **Faster dictation:** Cadence connects while the start sound plays and starts the app faster. When the only thing to tidy is an “um”, “uh” or a stutter like “I, I think”, Cadence fixes it on your PC and inserts straight away instead of waiting about a second for the cloud.
- When you start dictating and say nothing, or press the shortcut by accident, Cadence now just closes. Before, it showed “Couldn’t transcribe” with “No speech was detected” or “The recording couldn’t be used”.
- The dictation bubble’s waveform moves with a normal speaking voice. Before, the bars only came alive when you raised your voice.
- Includes Chat with Cadence from 1.2.10, Cadence in the notch and mouse side-button shortcuts from 1.2.9, and files in Refine from 1.2.8.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
