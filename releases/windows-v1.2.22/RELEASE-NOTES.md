# Cadence for Windows 1.2.22 — text that can't be inserted is copied for you

- **You no longer need to press Copy when Cadence can't insert.** If your dictation or a Refine result can't go into the app you're using (you switched apps, the text box changed, or the app didn't accept the paste), Cadence copies the text to your clipboard straight away and the card says so. Press Ctrl+V wherever you want it. Insert here and Retry insertion still work, and Copy is still there. When cleanup didn't finish, your raw transcript isn't copied until you choose Copy or Use raw transcript.
- Includes everything from 1.2.21: change your dictation shortcut from the sidebar, the small dictation pop-up, Cadence Agent in the Cadence window, and signing in returns to Cadence.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
