# Cadence for Windows 1.2.17 — sign in like on the Mac

- **Signing in now ends back in Cadence, as on the Mac.** When your browser hands the sign-in back, the welcome guide or Settings → Account comes back to the front while Cadence finishes setting up your account. Before, Windows kept the browser in front and Cadence only flashed in the taskbar, so pressing Allow looked like it did nothing even though sign-in had finished.
- **No more "Cadence for Mac wants to access Cadence" page.** That approval page came from the sign-in service, not from the app, and has been turned off for the Cadence apps. You see the Google, Apple, Microsoft or email sign-in page if your browser isn't signed in yet, then go straight back to Cadence.
- **Clearer usage messages.** On Pro, or with Free allowance left, a busy service now says "Cadence is still finishing another request. Try again in a moment." instead of sending you to your plan. A used-up Free allowance and the daily limit each say when they reset.
- Includes the stuck-key shortcut fix from 1.2.16, dictation with a picture on your clipboard from 1.2.15, dictation at the cursor from 1.2.14 and Cadence in the notch by default from 1.2.13.

1.2.18 and later include everything in this release; the in-app updater installs the latest version.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
