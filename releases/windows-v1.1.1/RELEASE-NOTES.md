Cadence 1.1.1 makes dashboard activity easier to read with a daily line graph.

- A connected line and visible points show daily word counts across the last seven days.
- Date labels, a zero-based scale and exact totals make rises and falls easy to understand.
- Select a point with the mouse or keyboard to inspect its daily total.
- Empty days stay at zero; no sample activity is added to your statistics.
- Retains the 1.1.0 account, dictation, Assistant, floating-control and updater behavior.

Windows 10 build 19041 or later, x64. Required runtimes are bundled. Existing preferences and account data remain outside the installation.

The installer remains unsigned. Fresh real-account sign-in, microphone/provider sessions and broad external-editor compatibility were not performed for this chart update. See the accompanying verification and provenance files for the exact automated checks.
