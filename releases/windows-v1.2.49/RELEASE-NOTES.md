# Cadence for Windows 1.2.49: Dictionary words come out right

- **Dictionary words that sound like ordinary words come out right:** say a word from your Dictionary such as "fal" and Cadence writes fal instead of "fell", only where you meant it ("I fell asleep" stays). When something you say sounds like one of your Dictionary words, Cadence's writing service reads the sentence and decides.
- **This works with cleanup & formatting off too:** then a dictation is sent to Cadence only when a word in it sounds like one of your enabled Dictionary words, together with those words, and only that word can change. Everything else is inserted exactly as you said it. The bubble says "Checking spelling…" while it checks; if Cadence takes more than 3 seconds, your text is inserted as heard.
- With "Clean up on this PC", a dictation with such a word is cleaned up by Cadence's writing service, which tells names from ordinary words better.
- Your settings, sign-in, History and chats are kept. Includes all features from 1.2.48.

Includes the required runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for exact checks and remaining limits.
