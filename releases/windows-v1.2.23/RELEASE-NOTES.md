# Cadence for Windows 1.2.23 — a simpler time-saved card

- **Home shows the time you've saved more simply.** The card leads with how much time speaking saved, then two bars compare how fast you write with Cadence and how fast you type, in words per minute. One line underneath says how many times faster that is, with your words and dictations. Set your own typing speed with the arrows next to it.
- Includes everything from 1.2.22: text that can't be inserted is copied for you, changing your dictation shortcut from the sidebar, the small dictation pop-up and Cadence Agent in the Cadence window.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
