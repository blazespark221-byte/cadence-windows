# Cadence for Windows 1.2.5 — your weekly dictation allowance on Home

- **See how much dictation you have left this week** at the top of Home: words left, a progress bar and when the allowance resets. It updates after every dictation. Pro shows Unlimited and your words this week.
- When the week's words or recording minutes run out, Home says so, shows when dictation resumes and offers Upgrade to Pro.
- Includes Cadence Agent's new character from 1.2.4, faster dictation cleanup from 1.2.2, and Google Docs dictation and direct browser sign-in from 1.2.1.

Includes the required .NET and Swift runtimes. The installer is unsigned. See RELEASE-VERIFICATION.md for the exact automated Windows checks and remaining limits.
